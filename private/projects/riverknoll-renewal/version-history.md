# Riverknoll Renewal: every version

Every model, deck and study we have made, oldest to newest, so anyone can see how the plan changed and why. Nothing is deleted: superseded versions stay here for checking. All are concept research by the initiative organizer, not an approved RIT project.

## Latest version (start here)

- **Feasibility model v24.1** (Nov 26, 2026): model v24 with System 3E's blocks stacked as fast as the maker says (145 square meters of wall in 1 day instead of 14), as the organizer chose. 744 beds, **$173.7M, $233,442 a bed**; **$294 a sq ft** for the buildings and **$575 a sq ft** all in ($299 and $584 for a two-story row); RIT's net share about $123.2M (71%). Adds the System 3E vs wood frame life-cycle tab.
- **Feasibility model v24.1, wood-frame plan** (Nov 26, 2026): the same model with 2x6 wood-frame walls, wood party walls and partitions, wood floor joists and advanced framing, kept so anyone can compare: **$167.6M, $225,240 a bed, $284 a sq ft** for the buildings, **$555** all in; RIT's share about $117.1M.
- **System 3E vs wood frame life cycle v2** (Nov 26, 2026): 30 and 50 year comparison. System 3E walls cost about $2,600 a bed more, save about $133 a bed a year and pay back in about year 28; the whole System 3E building with plank floors (about $7,800 a bed more) does not pay back within 50 years at 5.15%. Labor: about 13,100 fewer wall hours, about 1.3 weeks faster a phase.
- **Material list v2** (Nov 26, 2026): every material the design needs, nothing more, now including the 3-story homes: about **$162 a sq ft** to build and **$320 all in**, $130,049 a bed, from national installed list prices.
- **Pitch deck v26** (Nov 26, 2026) still shows the v24 numbers.

Everything below is an earlier version, kept so you can see how the work progressed.

Dollar figures are total project cost ("uses") in nominal dollars unless noted.
