## Start here: take the housing survey

Live at Riverknoll, or want to? Tell us what matters most to you in upperclass housing: rent, privacy, shared space, and what should stay. Your answers shape the next revision.

[Take the Riverknoll housing survey](https://forms.gle/ZZmocqQbpuf9BHMW8)

Riverknoll Renewal is a student-led proposal for affordable upperclass housing at RIT. RIT plans to close Riverknoll after the 2026–27 year. This page asks what it would take to rebuild it as affordable, all-electric townhomes, and shows all of our work, every version and every option, so anyone can check it and choose for themselves.

The initiative is independent. RIT has not approved or funded it. Figures are concept research and allowances, not bids. Updated November 26, 2026 (model v22, deck v25).

## What we propose now

| Item | Current plan |
| --- | --- |
| New beds | 744, sized from RIT's own enrollment and housing data (sourced range 444 to 871) |
| Homes | 18 identical rows of 10 townhomes, private bedrooms, one repeated design, built with System 3E prebuilt panels |
| Accessible beds | 72 single-level ground-floor beds, against about 38 required |
| Phasing | Design and permits first, a 72-bed pilot in fall 2029, then 4 rows a year, last homes 2033 |
| Total cost | About $159M, or $214k a bed |
| Old buildings | Assumed cleared by RIT's own decommissioning; our estimate of that is about $22.6M, shown separately and not in the total |

The bed count comes first and the design meets it. 744 beds would house about 53% of RIT undergraduates on campus, against a peer median of about 60%.

## Who could pay for it

Today's Riverknoll rent can repay about a quarter of the cost, whoever borrows. A nonprofit owner or private developer on an RIT ground lease changes who signs the loan, not the size of the gap.

| Scenario | RIT's share |
| --- | --- |
| RIT borrows (base), Riverknoll-level rent | About $109M |
| Other owners and loan terms, same rent | About $98M to $118M |
| Smaller peer-sized homes | About $83M |
| Plus rent near off-campus levels (about 1.5 times today) | About $48M |
| Plus every gift and grant at its high end | About $20M |

Grants and gifts we found add about $10.7M in the base case and up to $38.6M at best. Low-income housing tax credits and HUD college loans do not apply to student housing. The trade-off is clear: the less RIT pays, the more students pay or the smaller the homes.
