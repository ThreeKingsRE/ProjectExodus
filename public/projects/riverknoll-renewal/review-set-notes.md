# Riverknoll Renewal review materials

November 2026 concept studies, published by the initiative organizer. This package is not an approved RIT project, construction bid, or permit set.

- Architecture pin-up v10: conceptual room layouts, capacity cases, site constraints, and courtyard studies.
- Pitch deck v18: the earlier presentation of the concept.
- Feasibility workbook v11: the earlier financial and wall-energy screening model.
- Technical DXF v1: schematic geometry for review, not construction drawings.

## Important version distinctions

The pin-up's nominal four-bed, two-story layout tests a 28 by 36 foot footprint. That is 1,008 gross square feet per floor, or 2,016 per suite and about 504 per resident. The v11 cost workbook uses an independent allowance of 350 gross square feet per new bed. Its headline costs therefore do not price that larger drawn layout. The initiative's research notes explain this difference and show an area sensitivity separately.

The workbook's operative construction inputs are 350/425/525 dollars per gross square foot in Assumptions row 15. The descriptive source note in row 34 is outdated and lists different figures. Read the linked formulas and row 15 for the inputs that produced the displayed results.

Workbook v11 prices all 513 target beds as new until the retained-bed input is verified. The historic 513-bed count differs from the 444-bed figure in RIT's 2022 Campus Plan. Neither figure certifies the current inventory. Retained 516–536 beds must be deducted from the new-build count.

V11's approximately $30.4 million debt screen uses all modeled NOI for annual debt service. The later 1.20 coverage sensitivity shown in the initiative reduces supported debt to approximately $25.3 million. This sensitivity is not a v11 workbook result.

System 3E's local installed price and any upfront premium are unknown. The workbook's zero premium input is a placeholder. Its approximately $4,628 annual energy saving is an opaque-wall conduction screen, not a whole-building utility forecast or measured RIT saving. Campuswide financial references are not allocated Riverknoll costs.
